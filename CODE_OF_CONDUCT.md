# Code of Conduct

CLPRouter adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and project
events) and when someone represents the project in public.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, criticise ideas rather than people,
and do not harass anyone. Unacceptable behaviour includes sexualised language or imagery, personal or political
attacks, trolling, public or private harassment, and publishing others' private information without their
permission.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to a maintainer listed in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) (for example through
the contact details on their GitHub profile). Reports are handled confidentially. If a maintainer is involved,
contact another maintainer.

## Enforcement

Maintainers apply the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban or permanent
ban, depending on the impact of the behaviour. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may
face the same consequences.

Where CLPRouter contributors take part in LF Decentralized Trust or CLPR spaces, the code of conduct of those spaces
applies there as well.

Multi-hop routing for CLPR

CLPR joins two ledgers per Channel, with no bridge validators and no pooled liquidity. CLPRouter turns those Channels into a network: a message or payment hops ledger to ledger, every hop re-checks the sender's rules on-chain, and a receipt settles or refunds the escrow at the origin.

How it works

A routed message

Point-to-point Channels become a network. 100 ledgers need 100 Channels to a hub instead of 4,950 pairs, and each hop is a normal CLPR message verified by the receiving ledger.

Settle on Hedera

Pay on one chain, be paid on another, with the guarantee held on Hedera. Proofs only flow chain → Hedera, so it works with the verifiers that exist today.

The provider: a narrow role

A k-of-n committee (on testnet: a TEST committee, 3 of 5). Every action is public, signed and time-limited, with notice periods before it takes effect.

Can

  • Certify networks for ISO 20022, MiCA and Energy
  • Disable, and later re-enable, a malicious route
  • Blacklist an account after an exploit: its routed funds go to a locked quarantine vault

Cannot (enforced in the contracts)

  • Change, pause or upgrade Routers: no admin key, no pause
  • Move funds anywhere but the quarantine vault
  • Rewrite history or swap the registry
  • Release vault funds to itself
  • Choose routes: planning runs on the sender's side

Engineering note: Hedera's trace-size cap

Opening the Hedera side of the testnet Channel failed five times with INSUFFICIENT_GAS, at every gas limit from 5.6M to 15M, always with 4,062,470 gas used. eth_estimateGas and an anvil fork both succeeded.

The cause was not gas. After a contract call executes, a Hedera consensus node checks the size of the call trace it records (every call frame's input and output, precompile calls included) against contracts.maxSerializedTraceDataBytes, 262,144 bytes by default, and fails the transaction above it. The Channel config carried 512 committee keys (67 KB of calldata), repeated through the Service, its logic, the verifier and 257 BLS12-381 on-curve precompile checks: 301,836 bytes of raw trace. Neither the relay's gas estimate nor anvil applies the cap.

Fix: a testnet adapter stages the committee in 16 chunks of 32 keys, each checked on arrival and recorded as a Merkle subtree root; the config then names the 16 roots, and the unchanged CLPR verifier checks every bundle. The Channel opened and the route delivered. For relayers, Settle on Hedera caps bundles to Hedera at 12 messages, about 60% of the limit. A trace-size.mjs estimator in the repo checks every transaction before it is sent.
